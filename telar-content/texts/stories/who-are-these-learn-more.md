Sayri Túpac played a pivotal role in the consolidation of Spanish colonial rule by ending the active military resistance of the Neo-Inca State. As Sapa Inca of Vilcabamba, he negotiated a peaceful surrender with Viceroy Andrés Hurtado de Mendoza, leaving his mountain stronghold in 1558 to swear allegiance to the Spanish Crown, convert to Christianity, and integrate the indigenous and colonial elite [Sayri Tupac](https://140maeve.github.io/gumanpoma/iiif/objects/sayritupac/sayritupac.jpg)
